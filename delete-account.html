Platova Labs · Swift Invoice

Request account deletion

You can request deletion without reinstalling Swift Invoice.

  1. Email support@platovalabs.com from the email address registered to your Swift Invoice account.
  2. Use the subject Swift Invoice account deletion. State that you want your account and associated data deleted. Never include your password or payment-card details.
  3. Support will verify ownership and confirm the scope of deletion before processing your request. If you no longer have access to your account email, contact support for help verifying ownership.

Write deletion request

What the request covers

Your Swift Invoice login, profile and business details, stored customers, invoices and invoice items, payment records, and uploaded business logo. Save copies of invoices you need before requesting deletion. Copies you previously emailed or exported are not removed from their recipients or your device.

Delete selected data and keep your account

To request deletion of selected data, email support@platovalabs.com from your registered account email with the subject Swift Invoice data deletion. Identify the saved customers, invoices, payment records, business details or uploaded logo you want removed, and state that you want to keep your account. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.

Support will verify ownership and confirm the affected records before processing your request. Your login and data outside the confirmed scope remain in place. Deleting a saved customer alone does not remove that customer's details from previously saved invoice snapshots; include those invoices in your request if you also want their stored details removed.

You can also remove a saved customer from the customer picker when creating an invoice, or remove your uploaded logo from Invoice branding. Your account remains available. Existing exported or emailed copies are outside the app's control.

Subscriptions and purchase records

Account deletion does not cancel Google Play billing. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play. Google controls its own purchase records separately from Swift Invoice.

Processing and retained records

This is a support-assisted request, not immediate automatic deletion. Support will confirm completion and explain any records that must be retained, the reason, and the applicable retention period before completing the request. Contact support if you have questions about backups or retained records.

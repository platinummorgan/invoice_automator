Account and data deletion

Delete your account in the app

Open Swift Invoice, sign in, and choose Settings → Delete account. Confirm both prompts to permanently delete your login, profile and business details, customers, quotes, invoices and invoice items, payment records, uploaded logo, and job pictures. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Save copies of any documents you need first. Copies you previously emailed or exported are not removed from their recipients or your device.

If you cannot sign in

  1. Email support@platovalabs.com from the email address registered to your Swift Invoice account.
  2. Use the subject Swift Invoice account deletion. Never include your password or payment-card details.
  3. Support will verify ownership before processing the request.

Write deletion request

Delete selected data and keep your account

Email support@platovalabs.com from your registered account email and identify the records you want removed. Support will verify ownership and confirm the affected records.

Subscriptions and purchase records

Account deletion does not cancel store billing. Current Pro subscriptions are managed through Google Play. Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play. The store controls its own purchase records separately from Swift Invoice.

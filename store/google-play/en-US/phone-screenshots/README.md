# Google Play phone screenshots

Captured September 29, 2026 from Swift Invoice Android 1.3.0 (23), running on the connected Galaxy S24+. These are direct app captures at 1080 × 1920, without generated or composited UI. Temporary Android display sizing and system-bar settings were restored afterward.

Published order:

1. `01-workflow.png` — Quote → Invoice → Receipt menu.
2. `04-sample-invoice.png` — Built-in invoice preview using example customer/work details. Business phone/address and logo were hidden using unsaved preview controls.
3. `02-quote-builder.png` — Quote entry with an example customer and description.
4. `05-quote-pricing.png` — Example line price and quote validity controls.
5. `03-invoice-design.png` — Layout, accent color and business-details positioning.

No customer records, real financial reports, contact details or purchase tokens are included. Example quote fields were restored to their original blank/zero values after capture; no quote or customer was saved to the backend. Preview design changes were not saved.

Google validated and committed these five images to the `en-US` phone screenshot listing, replacing six images of the older interface. A fresh API read confirmed the count and order. The old images and listing metadata are backed up locally under the ignored `.validation/store-screenshots-20260929/previous` directory. Public storefront propagation/review may lag the accepted update.

No app binary or Apple listing was changed by this screenshot update.
